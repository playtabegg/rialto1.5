Rialto 1.5.1: the tools it carries
==================================

Rialto is MIT licensed (see LICENSE). Rialto.exe also carries three programs
made by other people, so that nobody has to install them. Rialto runs each one
as a separate program. None of them is changed. If Inno Setup or the Windows
ADK is already installed on your PC, Rialto uses yours and leaves these alone.

Inside Rialto.exe they sit in helpers\inno and helpers\cdrtools. Rialto runs
only these copies, never a helpers folder put beside Rialto.exe.


1. Inno Setup 6.5.4 (helpers\inno)
----------------------------------
What it does:   compiles the installer (setup.exe) for your game.
Made by:        Jordan Russell and Martijn Laan. https://jrsoftware.org/
License:        the Inno Setup License. Its text is in InnoSetup-license.txt
                here and in helpers\inno\license.txt.
Source:         https://github.com/jrsoftware/issrc (tag is-6_5_4)
Files carried:  ISCC.exe, ISCmplr.dll, ISPP.dll, ISPPBuiltins.iss, Setup.e32,
                SetupLdr.e32, islzma.dll, Default.isl, license.txt, the five
                .issig signature files, and 20 files in Languages\.
                Unmodified, from the signed 6.5.4 release.

The license lets anyone use and pass on Inno Setup, for any purpose. Inno
Setup's makers ask businesses and freelancers who earn more than USD 5,000 a
year to buy a commercial license: https://jrsoftware.org/isorder.php
That is a request, not a condition of the license. Rialto's log may show
Inno Setup's "Non-commercial use only" line. It is about this request. The
installers it makes carry no such line.


2. mkisofs 3.02a10 (helpers\cdrtools\mkisofs.exe)
-------------------------------------------------
What it does:   writes the disc image (.iso).
Made by:        Joerg Schilling and the cdrtools authors (Eric Youngdale,
                James Pearson, HELIOS Software GmbH and others).
Build:          "mkisofs 3.02a10 2021/07/23 (i686-pc-cygwin)", the Windows
                build that cdrtfe ships, taken unmodified from cdrtfe's
                portable zip (cdrtfe-1.5.9.1.zip, SHA-256
                86a6d8ad02351303ab2ec6dcfa9b2e97514a2a6586751adabc5c6ca10e117e12).
                Rialto's mkisofs.exe and cygwin1.dll match the two files in
                that zip byte for byte (checked 18 October 2026).
License:        mkisofs is under the GNU General Public License, version 2
                or (at your option) any later version. Its text is in
                GPL-2.0.txt here and in helpers\cdrtools. Parts of cdrtools
                built into it (the schily libraries) are under the Common
                Development and Distribution License (CDDL) 1.0, whose
                text is in CDDL-1.0.txt here and in helpers\cdrtools
                (taken from the source's own CDDL.Schily.txt).
Source:         cdrtfe names the source this build came from:
                https://cdrtfe.sourceforge.io/tools/index_en.html
                https://sourceforge.net/projects/cdrtfe/files/tools/source/cdrtools/schily-2021-09-18.tar.bz2
                https://sourceforge.net/projects/cdrtfe/files/tools/source/cdrtools/cdrtools-patch.rar
                Upstream (schilytools):
                https://sourceforge.net/projects/schilytools/files/
                https://codeberg.org/schilytools/schilytools


3. Cygwin DLL 2.3.1 (helpers\cdrtools\cygwin1.dll)
--------------------------------------------------
What it does:   lets mkisofs run on Windows. mkisofs does nothing without it.
Made by:        the Cygwin authors (Red Hat, Inc. and others).
                https://cygwin.com/
License:        the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your
                option) any later version. Its text is in GPL-3.0.txt here
                and in helpers\cdrtools. Cygwin moved to the LGPL only with
                version 2.5.2 (June 2016); this is 2.3.1 (November 2015).
Source:         the Cygwin source at the tag cygwin-2_3_1-release:
                https://sourceware.org/git/?p=newlib-cygwin.git;a=tag;h=refs/tags/cygwin-2_3_1-release
                (a mirror: https://github.com/mirror/newlib-cygwin/tree/cygwin-2_3_1-release)
                The release announcement:
                https://sourceware.org/ml/cygwin-announce/2015-11/msg00042.html
                Older Cygwin packages, source included (cygwin-2.3.1-1-src):
                http://www.crouchingtigerhiddenfruitbat.org/Cygwin/timemachine.html


Getting the source from us
--------------------------
We The Indies publishes the corresponding source of mkisofs (schily-2021-09-18
and cdrtfe's patch) and of cygwin1.dll 2.3.1 beside each Rialto release that
carries them, at https://github.com/playtabegg/rialto1.5/releases. For at
least three years from each such release, anyone may also ask us for a copy
of that source through https://github.com/playtabegg/rialto1.5/issues, at no
charge beyond the cost of sending it.
